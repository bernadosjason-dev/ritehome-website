Questions people in CDO actually ask

Do you build custom cabinets and wardrobes in CDO?

Yes. Walk-in wardrobes, TV consoles, open shelving and storage walls are drawn to the exact wall, corner and ceiling height, then built off site and installed as one system.

Can a wardrobe be designed around an odd-shaped room?

That's the point of drawing before building — the elevation is measured against your actual walls, not a standard module width, so an alcove, a sloped ceiling or a corner turn is drawn in, not worked around afterward.

What's the difference between a walk-in closet and a wardrobe run?

A wardrobe run is a single wall of storage; a walk-in closet is a small room of it — rails, shelving and drawers on more than one wall. Both are drawn and quoted the same way, board by board.

Do you also do TV consoles and feature walls?

Yes — TV console walls, backlit feature panels and open shelving towers are part of the storage system, often specified alongside a wardrobe in the same project.

Are wardrobes and storage cabinets treated against termites?

Yes — a wardrobe is one of the worst places for a termite problem to go unnoticed, so every board is treated against termites before assembly, and every edge is closed with edgebanding, not left as raw exposed core.
