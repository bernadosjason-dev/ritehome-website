Hero background loop — slide 01 (Graphite Gloss)
================================================

graphite-gloss-loop.mp4   (in use)
  Source : C:\Users\JB\Downloads\IMG_2388.MOV  — a REAL phone video of the
           black-gloss glass-front kitchen (the actual installation the
           slide-01 still shows). Supplied by Jason 2026-09-10 to replace
           the earlier AI/Veo clip: "its better quality".
  Original: 1080x1920 portrait, ~54 fps, 30.8 MB, H.264, moov atom at the
           end (no faststart), rotate=90 metadata.
  Encoded : ffmpeg -an -r 30 -vf scale=1080:1920 -c:v libx264 -profile high
           -pix_fmt yuv420p -preset slow -crf 24 -metadata:s:v:0 rotate=0
           -movflags +faststart   ->  1080x1920, 30 fps, no audio,
           faststart (moov first), 2.6 MB.
  ffmpeg came from the npm package ffmpeg-static (no ffmpeg on this box
  otherwise); scratchpad/vid/ in the session that made this.

How it is wired (ritehome-website.html) — unchanged from before:
  <video class="photo-vid" autoplay muted loop playsinline preload="none"
         poster="ritehome-photos/enhanced/01-graphite-gloss.jpg"
         data-src="ritehome-photos/reel/graphite-gloss-loop.mp4">
  Sits over the <img>, under the .photo::after scrim. JS sets src on first
  reach and pauses it whenever slide 01 is off screen. object-fit:cover ->
  the 9:16 clip is cropped to a centre band (counter + island) on a wide
  screen. @media (prefers-reduced-motion) hides the video, the still shows.

any more — but the slide-01 still and this clip should stay the same
