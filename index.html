18 — Brief

Renovation anxiety is rarely about taste. It is about not knowing.

Not knowing what the number covers. Not knowing whether the finish on the last cabinet will match the first. Not knowing what changed between the day you agreed and the day it arrived. We built the company around removing that, which is why the drawing and the itemised quantities come before the price, and why nothing is fabricated on your floor.

19 — Sequence

Five stages, each with a name attached.

A job moves through five tracked stages. At any point you can ask which stage yours is in and who is holding it, and get an answer rather than an estimate.

01

Consultation

Consultant

Site visit booked, the room and budget discussed, and a schedule agreed. Ends when a visit date is set.

02

Procurement officer

Boards, edging, hardware and accessories bought against the approved bill of quantities.

Installer

Delivered finished, levelled and set. Your floor sees assembly, not a workshop.

Start with the measurement.

22 — FAQ

Kitchen cabinets and renovation in CDO, answered plainly.

The questions we hear most from people planning a kitchen renovation or new cabinetry in Cagayan de Oro.

Do you build kitchen cabinets in CDO?

Yes. Kitchen cabinets in Cagayan de Oro (CDO) are our core line — modular, itemised board by board, drawn and quoted before a single sheet is cut, then built off site by our own crew and installed on your floor.

Do you handle full kitchen renovation projects in Cagayan de Oro?

Yes. A CDO kitchen renovation with Ritehome runs through five tracked stages — consultation, design, procurement, fabrication and installation — with the drawing and an itemised quantity list approved before anything is fabricated.

What does CDO cabinetry from Ritehome include?

Our cabinetry in CDO covers kitchens, wardrobes, storage walls, partitions and office fit-outs, drawn to the millimetre and available in gloss, matte, woodgrain or solid-colour board finishes.

Where is Ritehome Modular Systems located?

Our showroom is at the E & J Building, Apovel, Cagayan de Oro City, Misamis Oriental. Delivery is free within the first 50 km of Cagayan de Oro.
