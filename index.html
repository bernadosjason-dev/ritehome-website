09 — Module

Modular does not
mean less custom.

It means the carcass is built to your dimensions in the shop, on a bench, under controlled conditions — then carried in finished and set. Backs are the same 18 mm board as the carcass, not a 3 mm skin. It costs more, and it is the reason a cabinet still sits square after it has been loaded.

11 — Brief

Renovation anxiety is rarely about taste. It is about not knowing.

Not knowing what the number covers. Not knowing whether the finish on the last cabinet will match the first. Not knowing what changed between the day you agreed and the day it arrived. We built the company around removing that, which is why the drawing and the itemised quantities come before the price, and why nothing is fabricated on your floor.

12 — Ledger

A quotation you can audit.

Your quotation is not a lump sum with a signature line. It is a bill of quantities: every board, every metre of edgebanding, every hinge and every accessory, listed with its thickness, its dimensions and the quantity your drawing consumes.

It is organised in the same six sections every time, so a quotation from us can be read against a quotation from anyone else — line by line, rather than number against number.

15 — Enquiry

Start with the measurement.

Tell us the room and where you are. A site visit is arranged from there, and the drawing follows the visit.

Three measurements worth having ready before the first call: the full length of each wall the run meets, the ceiling height, and the position of any window, door or service point along that wall.
