Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This page covers two things: how Ritehome Modular Systems ("Ritehome," "we," "us") handles personal data collected on ritehomemodular.com and through Hannah, our chat widget, and the terms that apply to using this site. If anything here is unclear, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

Ritehome Modular Systems is a modular cabinetry and interior fit-out business based at E & J Building, Apovel, Cagayan de Oro City, Misamis Oriental, Philippines.

What personal data we collect

  • The inquiry form (the "Start with the measurement" section): your name, phone number, email address, site location, and any notes you write about your project.
  • Hannah, our chat widget: the messages you type; if you choose to give it, a phone number or email so a team member can follow up; and if you choose to attach one, a reference photo of your space for a rough size estimate.
  • We do not currently run analytics or advertising tracking (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) on this site. If that changes, we'll update this policy first.

Why we collect it

To respond to your inquiry, arrange a site visit, prepare a quotation, and answer your questions about our products and process. We don't use your data for anything beyond that.

Who we share it with

  • Inquiry form submissions and any phone/email Hannah collects are stored in a private Google Sheet (via Google Apps Script), for our own follow-up and record-keeping.
  • Hannah's replies are generated by one of a few AI providers (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or Cloudflare Workers AI, depending on which is active) — your message, and any photo you attach, is sent to that provider to generate a reply. Cloudflare hosts Hannah's backend itself.
  • When a real person needs to follow up on a chat, a summary of your message (and a phone/email, if you gave one) is sent to our private internal Telegram account. This is never made public.
  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and lead records for as long as reasonably needed to follow up with you and to recognise you if you contact us again. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see Contact below.

Your rights

Under the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to be informed about how your data is processed, to access it, to request correction or erasure, to object to processing, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your rights have been violated. Contact us first using the details below and we'll do our best to sort it out directly.

Third-party services on this site

This site embeds a Google Map (on the Contact section) and loads Google Fonts — both send your browser's request, including your IP address, to Google, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy. The WhatsApp, Viber, and Facebook links on this site take you to those companies' own platforms, governed by their own privacy policies, once you click them.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's involvement.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the latest version.
